# EarBud 0.1.0

The first release of EarBud, a Mac app for the Fieldy 3 wristband recorder.
EarBud copies recordings from your Fieldy to your Mac over Bluetooth and
transcribes them on your Mac. No phone app, no account, no cloud.

## Install

1. Download `EarBud-0.1.0.dmg` below.
2. Open it and drag EarBud to Applications.
3. Open EarBud and follow the setup. It shows you each step, with pictures.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later.

## What it does

- Guided setup: reset, pair, and set the clock on your Fieldy.
- One-click sync. Each recording is saved the moment it arrives, so an
  interrupted sync loses nothing.
- Transcription on your Mac with Whisper. Audio is never uploaded.
- Recordings and transcripts by day, with playback.
- A daily sync reminder and a menu-bar status.
- An optional dock around the MacBook camera notch, or a floating capsule on
  Macs without a notch.
- Import audio from other recorders (Voice Memos, RØDE Central exports and
  others): drag files or folders onto the window or the Dock icon, or use
  File > Import Audio….

## Before you start

- Setup factory-resets the Fieldy so it pairs with your Mac. After that, the
  official Fieldy phone app no longer sees it.
- The Fieldy deletes a recording once it has sent it. EarBud keeps the only
  copy, on your Mac.
- English transcription only in this version.
- EarBud does not change the Fieldy's firmware. Nothing is opened, soldered,
  or flashed.
