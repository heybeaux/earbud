1 Before macOS asks

EarBud needs Bluetooth

Your Fieldy sends its recordings to this Mac over Bluetooth. When you click Allow Bluetooth, macOS will ask you to confirm.

EarBud only connects to your Fieldy. It doesn't use Bluetooth for anything else.

2 If permission was denied

EarBud can't reach your Fieldy without Bluetooth

  1. Click Open System Settings below.
  2. In Privacy & Security, click Bluetooth.
  3. Turn on the switch next to EarBud.
  4. Come back here. EarBud carries on by itself.
