Welcome to EarBud

EarBud copies the recordings from your Fieldy 3 to this Mac and turns them into text you can read and search.

Your recordings, on your MacPress the button on your Fieldy and talk. When you're back at your desk, click Sync.
Stays on this MacYour audio and its text never leave this Mac. No account, no cloud.
Not made by Field LabsEarBud is an independent app that works with Fieldy 3. Field Labs doesn't make or support it.
